The MDS Specialist Advantage

Why trust an MDS Prosthodontist & Implantologist with your missing teeth?

A dental implant is not just a titanium screw in the bone — its long-term success depends on how accurately the crown on top distributes chewing forces across your jaw. As an MDS Prosthodontist & Implantologist, Dr. Prakruti Shah has specialized master's training in both implant surgery and prosthetic crown engineering.

01

Prosthetically Driven Implant Placement

Every implant is positioned based on the exact angle and bite of your future crown, preventing screw loosening, food trapping, or bone loss.

02

Zero Damage to Adjacent Healthy Teeth

Unlike traditional tooth-supported bridges, a single dental implant replaces your missing tooth without grinding down healthy neighbouring teeth.

03

Biocompatible Titanium & Metal-Free Zirconia

We use globally trusted FDA/CE-certified implant systems paired with lifelike, high-translucency CAD/CAM Zirconia crowns.

Step-by-Step Clinical Journey

How Dental Implant treatment works at The Dental Den.

STEP 01

Digital X-Ray & 3D CBCT Planning

Dr. Prakruti Shah evaluates your bone density, gum health, and bite alignment to map exact implant dimensions before touching a tooth.

STEP 02

Painless Keyhole Implant Placement

Under gentle local anaesthesia, the titanium implant is placed precisely into the jawbone in 30–45 minutes with minimal discomfort.

STEP 03

Osseointegration (Bone Bonding)

Over 8–12 weeks (or immediately in eligible immediate-load cases), your natural bone fuses solidly around the implant surface.

STEP 04

Custom CAD/CAM Zirconia Crown

A digital impression is taken and your custom-shaded Zirconia crown is torqued onto the implant — restoring 100% natural chewing power.
