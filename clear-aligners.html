How Clear Aligners Work

4 simple steps from digital scan to your straight smile.

STEP 01

Bite Assessment & Digital Scan

Dr. Prakruti examines your teeth, gums, and jaw joints and captures a high-precision digital scan without messy putty impressions.

STEP 02

3D Movement Simulation

Preview how your teeth will move week by week on screen and know your exact number of aligner trays before beginning treatment.

STEP 03

Wear & Switch Every 10–14 Days

Wear each custom tray 20–22 hours a day, switching to the next sequential set at home while visiting the clinic every 4–6 weeks for progress checks.

STEP 04

Smile Reveal & Retainers

Once your teeth reach their final alignment, we polish your enamel and fit custom clear retainers to preserve your new smile permanently.
