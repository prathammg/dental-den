Dr. Prakruti Shah (MDS, Prosthodontist & Implantologist)

The Dental Den is the brainchild of Dr. Prakruti Shah (MDS, Prosthodontist & Implantologist). With more than 8 years of dedicated clinical practice, Dr. Prakruti founded The Dental Den in Prabhadevi with a clear vision: to create a calm, modern, women-owned dental studio where patients receive specialist-grade dentistry without the rush or anxiety of commercial clinic chains.

As a postgraduate specialist in Prosthodontics and Implantology, Dr. Prakruti's core training centres on the most demanding restorative procedures in dentistry — including single and full-arch Dental Implants, Full-Mouth Rehabilitation, Smile Designing, Porcelain Laminates & Veneers, precision Crowns & Bridges, Complete & Partial Dentures, and Maxillofacial Prosthetics.
