Save Your Natural Tooth

5 warning signs your tooth needs immediate root canal relief.

When deep decay, a cracked filling, or trauma reaches the inner nerve chamber (dental pulp) of a tooth, simple fillings can no longer resolve the infection. Root Canal Treatment gently removes the inflamed nerve tissue while keeping your natural tooth root anchored in your jaw.

01

Lingering Pain to Hot Tea, Coffee, or Cold Water

Sharp sensitivity that continues throbbing for several seconds or minutes even after the hot or cold drink is gone.

02

Spontaneous Night-Time Toothache

Deep, pulsing pain that wakes you up at night or worsens when lying down and radiates toward your ear, temple, or jaw.

03

Pain When Biting Down or Chewing

Tenderness when tapping or chewing on a specific tooth, or a small pimple-like swelling (sinus tract) on the gum near the root tip.

Our 4-Step Painless Protocol

What happens during your Root Canal at The Dental Den.

STEP 01

Digital RVG X-Ray & Topical Numbing

We take an instant low-radiation digital X-ray to map the root canals, apply surface numbing gel, and administer painless local anaesthesia.

STEP 02

Rotary Cleaning & Apex Locator

Quiet, flexible Nickel-Titanium (NiTi) rotary files and electronic apex locators clean out 100% of bacteria from the canals without hand-filing discomfort.

STEP 03

3D Biocompatible Sealing & Core

Once completely disinfected, the root canals are hermetically sealed with biocompatible gutta-percha and reinforced with a high-strength composite core.

STEP 04

Custom Prosthodontist Crown

Dr. Prakruti Shah (MDS Prosthodontist) designs a custom Zirconia or E-max crown that locks over the treated tooth so it never cracks under chewing load.
