Step-by-Step Clinical Process

How a 45-minute Hair PRP session works at our Prabhadevi clinic.

STEP 01

Small Blood Draw

A small sample of your own blood (similar to a routine blood test) is collected using sterile, single-use medical vacutainers.

STEP 02

Double-Spin Centrifugation

The sample is spun in a medical centrifuge to separate golden Platelet-Rich Plasma containing 4–5x concentrated natural growth factors.

STEP 03

Topical Scalp Numbing

Your scalp is cleansed and numbed with soothing anaesthetic cream so the entire session feels calm and pain-free.

STEP 04

Targeted Micro-Activation

Dr. Prakruti Shah delivers the concentrated growth factors directly to thinning hair follicle roots — with zero downtime afterward.

Hair PRP FAQs

Common questions about Hair PRP Therapy in Prabhadevi.

Hair PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) uses your own small blood sample spun in a sterile centrifuge to concentrate natural growth-factor platelets. Because nothing synthetic or foreign is injected, there is zero risk of allergic reaction.
Hair PRP at The Dental Den is a comfortable, pain-free outpatient procedure performed after applying topical numbing cream on the scalp. There is zero downtime — you can return to work in Prabhadevi immediately after your session.
While reduced hair shedding is often noticed after the very first or second visit, most patients achieve optimal density and thickness with a course of 4 to 6 monthly sessions, followed by periodic maintenance if needed.
